NanoPlayer · Test bench

The core through its public API, exactly as an integrator would use it: createPlayer() and the lifecycle methods, one step at a time.

The control bar is here too. Try it with the keyboard only: Tab to move, Space or K to play, arrows to seek and change the volume, M to mute, F for full screen. The lifecycle buttons are deliberately raw: they are what this page is here to show.

With two streams the synchronizer is running. Press Desync by 400 ms or Seek at random and watch the drift close: Loop action shows whether it corrects smoothly or with a hard seek. The timecodes burned into the videos let you check it by eye.
